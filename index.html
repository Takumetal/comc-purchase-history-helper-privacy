COMC Purchase History Helper

Privacy policy

Last updated: September 23, 2026 · Operator: Takumetal

日本語English

COMC Purchase History Helper is a Chrome extension that checks manually imported purchase history on your device. It is an unofficial tool and is not affiliated with COMC.

1. Data handled

The extension reads the CSV file you select and displays a preview. It saves the following purchase records when you confirm the import. Selecting a file alone does not save purchase records.

  • ItemID, sport, set name, card number and description
  • Serial number, quantity manufactured, purchase price, date and time recorded in the CSV, and Return flag
  • Import time, counts and date coverage, information needed for matching and deduplication, warnings and conflicting record candidates

Reading the CSV temporarily involves the seller column (Purchased From) and unknown columns, but their values are not saved in purchase records. The raw CSV, selected file name and file path are not stored either.

Your language and badge settings are saved locally. When language is set to Automatic, the extension reads Chrome's display language.

2. Purpose and product page access

Data is used to save and search purchase history, match card types, review duplicates and conflicts, back up and restore records, and apply display settings. Purchase history does not confirm current ownership.

On supported public COMC product pages, the extension reads displayed product information, including sport, set, card number, description, print run and product links, to check local history. Page URLs and tab information are used temporarily to identify supported pages and process them; they are not saved as browsing history.

The extension does not log in to COMC, automatically download purchase history or crawl pages. Account, Inventory and checkout pages are excluded. It has no function to collect cookies, passwords, tokens or payment credentials.

Our use of information handled by this extension complies with the Limited Use requirements of the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy. Information is used only for the purchase-history features and display settings described above, and not for advertising, data sales, creditworthiness or lending decisions.

3. Storage and protection

Purchase history and settings are stored in the extension's local storage in the Chrome profile you use. The extension does not sync them to the cloud. Access to the full history is restricted to the extension's trusted contexts.

The extension does not encrypt stored data or exported backups. It does not prevent access by people or software that can read your device, Chrome profile or saved files.

4. Transmission and page visibility

The extension does not send CSV files, purchase history, viewed products or diagnostics to the operator or external servers. It uses no analytics, advertising, external AI services or automatic error reporting. Normal use does not provide the operator with data stored on your device.

Badges added to COMC pages may be visible to scripts on those pages. They may allow the page to infer that you have a purchase record or possible match for the displayed card. Purchase prices, dates, counts, purchased ItemIDs and the full history are not included in badges or responses to the page content script.

Badges are ON for new installations. Turning off “Show badges on COMC pages” stops adding history status to the page; you can still check it inside the extension. Turning badges off does not delete history, and displayed product information is still read.

COMC and Chrome make their own network connections independently of this extension. External services are governed by their own terms and privacy policies.

5. Retention and deletion

History is kept until you delete it or uninstall the extension. Shipping cards, logging out of COMC, disabling the extension or turning badges off does not delete it. Older records absent from a newer CSV are retained. Importing CSV files from different COMC accounts mixes their histories.

“Delete history” removes purchase records, conflicts and import information while keeping settings. “Delete all extension data” also resets settings. Uninstalling removes the extension's local data.

These actions do not delete original CSV files, exported backups, or copies made by you or your operating system. Delete those at their saved locations. The extension does not provide forensic secure erasure.

6. Backup and restore

On your request, the extension exports a JSON file containing purchase history, conflicts, settings and import information. It is not encrypted; keep it out of shared folders and public locations. Cloud syncing configured for your chosen location is outside the extension's control.

Restoring also requires manual file selection, a preview and confirmation. The default is to merge with existing history. Replacement overwrites existing history after an additional confirmation.
